Empowering Madhya Pradesh Since 1955

Govt. Div. Industrial Training Institute (ITI) Gwalior is a premier government technical education center. It is strictly affiliated with the National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT), New Delhi, and recognized by the Directorate General of Training (DGT), Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship.

Principal's Message

"Our mission is to bridge the gap between industry requirements and academic learning. Through hands-on training, modern workshops, and dedicated faculty, we forge the future workforce of India, fully aligned with the Skill India initiative."

  • State-of-the-art Computer & Electronics Labs
  • Highly equipped Mechanical Workshops
  • 100% Placement Assistance

Directorate General of Training (DGT)

Working under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), Government of India, the DGT formulates policies, sets standards, and standardizes vocational training across the nation to ensure industry-ready graduates.

NCVT & SCVT Affiliation

Our courses are strictly affiliated with the National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT). Graduates receive the National Trade Certificate (NTC), which is globally recognized for employment in public and private sectors.

Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS)

Implemented to ensure a steady flow of skilled workers in different trades for the domestic industry. CTS reduces unemployment by providing hands-on, practical engineering and non-engineering skills to the youth.

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1

    Online Registration

    Register on the official MP Online ITI Counseling Portal. Create your profile and pay the registration fee.

  • 2

    Choice Filling

    Select 'Govt. Div. ITI Gwalior' as your first preference and choose your desired trades in order of priority.

  • 3

    Merit List & Allotment

    Wait for the DGT merit list. Selection is strictly based on 10th/8th board marks percentage.

  • 4

    Document Verification

    Report to the ITI campus with original documents (Aadhaar, Marksheets, Domicile, Caste Certificate) to finalize admission.
